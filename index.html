CyberLawPal — Cybercrime RAG System

A retrieval-augmented generation assistant that answers cybercrime law questions under Pakistan's PECA 2016. Semantic search runs on FAISS with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings, generation is handled by Groq-hosted LLMs, and the whole thing is wrapped in a Gradio interface anyone can actually use.
